Interactive tool · Kansas Public Finance Center

Fiscal Shocker

An interactive tool that compares the fiscal health, revenue structure, and spending priorities of local governments across Kansas. It standardizes key financial indicators, such as property tax reliance, so that one jurisdiction can be read against another.

The tool is meant for policymakers, researchers, and engaged residents who need local government finances to be clear and comparable.

Interactive tool · taxpayer receipt

{{TOOL_NAME}}

An interactive taxpayer receipt for Wichita Public Schools (USD 259). A resident enters the appraised value of a home and sees the school district property tax it owes and the services that tax pays for, levy by levy.

The Kansas homestead exemption applies to the 20-mill general levy only, so a single multiplier gets every bill wrong. The tool computes each levy separately, and the estimate holds at every home value.

Commissioned studies

  • 2025 Florida Property Tax Study Commissioned by the Florida League of Cities · conducted at Wichita State University Florida is debating changes to the homestead property tax. The study examines the fiscal, distributional, and community-level impacts of the concepts under discussion, and what they would mean for the revenue structure of Florida municipalities. The full report is Guo and Cheng (2025), Fiscal Structure and Revenue Resilience of Florida Municipalities. Study page at the Florida League of Cities Summary of key findings (PDF) Full report (PDF)
