Hai (David) Guo

Regents Distinguished Professor of Public Finance
Director, Kansas Public Finance Center
Hugo Wall School of Public Affairs, Wichita State University

State and local public finance—how governments raise, budget, and report public money.

Hai (David) Guo is the Regents Distinguished Professor of Public Finance at the Hugo Wall School of Public Affairs, Wichita State University, where he also directs the Kansas Public Finance Center. He joined Wichita State in 2022 after fourteen years on the faculty of Florida International University. He holds a Ph.D. in public policy from the joint program of Georgia State University and the Georgia Institute of Technology.

His research examines how state and local governments raise, budget, and report public money. He has published more than 30 peer-reviewed articles in journals such as the Journal of Public Administration Research and Theory, Public Budgeting & Finance, International Tax and Public Finance, and the American Review of Public Administration.

His work is also applied. The center’s Fiscal Shocker, an interactive tool, compares the fiscal health, revenue structure, and spending priorities of Kansas local governments. He has worked with Sedgwick County, the City of Kechi, and Wichita Public Schools on revenue analysis, financial planning, and budget simulations that engage residents. He serves on the State of Kansas Consensus Revenue Estimation Group, on the Wichita Police and Fire Retirement Pension Board, and as a council member of the City of Maize, Kansas, and he is the Chair-Elect of the Association for Budgeting and Financial Management. The aim of this applied work is to give budget directors, local leaders, and residents information they can use in fiscal decisions.
