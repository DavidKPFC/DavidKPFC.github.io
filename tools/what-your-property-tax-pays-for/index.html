      About this tool. Built by Hai (David) Guo from the district’s published budget documents. It is not an official product of Wichita Public Schools.

      Sources. Mill rates and fund totals come from the USD 259 2026–27 budget documents (Notice of Hearing 9/14/2026). The service categories, and the share of each levy assigned to each service, were prepared for this receipt.

      How the split works. Each levy’s tax is computed exactly — the exemption is applied to the first 20 mills only — then divided among services by a fixed share for that levy. A service paid by more than one levy is shown once, with those levies added together. Because the exemption shrinks the General Fund’s weight on lower-valued homes, the mix changes with the home value.

      What this is not. An estimate for residential property, not a tax bill. It ignores delinquency, special assessments, protests and any exemption other than the residential property exemption from the statewide school mill levy, and it covers only the school district’s levies — not the county, city or state. Tax dollars are not earmarked to particular services inside a fund; the split here is pro rata, not a tracing.
